# USYC — Official Pricing & Valuation Methodology

**Researched:** 2026-08-05 · **All prices observed live 2026-08-05** (latest price report is dated 2026-08-04)
**Primary sources:** Circle/Hashnote official docs (`usyc.docs.hashnote.com`), Circle developer docs, circle.com/usyc, the live USYC public API, and direct `eth_call` reads of the USYC Oracle on Ethereum mainnet.

---

## WHAT TO SAY ON STAGE (only facts confirmed against primary sources)

1. **USYC is an accruing-NAV token, not a $1.00 rebasing coin** — price = NAV of the fund's prime-brokerage account ÷ total USYC supply, so yield shows up as a **rising token price**. Today it is **$1.13306696** (round 483, reported 2026-08-04).
2. **The price updates once per business day, typically ~9am ET**, after the prime broker reconciles the prior day's activity — Mon–Fri, excluding US Federal Holidays.
3. **2:00pm ET is a real, documented cutoff — but it governs SUBSCRIPTIONS only.** After 2:00pm ET you subscribe at a *forecast* of tomorrow's price; **redemptions always execute at the current published price, 24/7/365.**
4. **The price reaches chain via a Circle/Hashnote-operated oracle that implements the Chainlink `AggregatorV3` interface** — it is *not* a Chainlink decentralized network, not NAVLink, not RedStone. There is also a **public, unauthenticated JSON API**: `https://usyc.hashnote.com/api/price`.
5. **USYC is live on Canton** (announced Oct 2024, with Digital Asset) — Circle lists Canton alongside Ethereum, BNB, NEAR and Solana. *(Caveat: Circle publishes no Canton-specific contract or price mechanics — see UNVERIFIED.)*

> **On the 2pm question specifically, the safe stage phrasing is:**
> *"Subscriptions after 2pm ET are priced off the next business day's report; redemptions are always at the current price."*
> Do **not** say "all trades after 2pm ET price at tomorrow's NAV" — that is wrong for redemptions.

## 1. The daily strike / cutoff time — **CONFIRMED (with an important correction)**

### VERDICT: **2:00pm ET is CONFIRMED** — but it is a *subscription-only* cutoff, not a universal strike time.

**Source A — Circle/Hashnote official docs, "Token Price" page**

Exact quote:

```
## Effective Subscription Price

Redemptions always occur at the current USYC price, and Subscriptions occur at the
Effective Subscription Price. This aligns subscriptions with whether or not the capital
was able to be included in earning yield for that business day.
```

## 2. How the price is calculated — **accruing NAV, CONFIRMED**

### It is an accruing NAV. It is NOT a constant $1.00 with distributed dividends.

**Source — Circle/Hashnote "Token Price"** · https://usyc.docs.hashnote.com/overview/token-price · 2026-08-05

```
## Token Price Calculation

USYC price is calculated based on the Net Asset Value (NAV) of the Prime Brokerage account
divided by the total supply of USYC on issue

As new investors enter the fund, they buy in at the current price per share.
```

**Source — circle.com/usyc** (official Circle product page) · Fetched 2026-08-05

> "USYC yield accrues automatically through a rising token price"
> "USYC token balance, yield, and price are streamed to a transparent oracle feed."

**Source — Circle developer docs** · https://developers.circle.com/tokenized/usyc/overview · 2026-08-05

```
* **Yield profile**: Assets earn the overnight federal funds rate through reverse repo
  agreements.
* **Transparency**: The token price and fund holdings are published through an onchain
  Oracle.
```

### I verified the NAV formula numerically against live data

Round 483 (the current round) from `https://usyc.hashnote.com/api/price-reports`:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `balance` (NAV) | 3,008,198,120.68 |
| `totalSupply` | 2,654,890,748.199602 |
| `balance / totalSupply` | **1.133077932762390** |
| reported `price` | **1.133066960425761961** |

The formula reproduces the published price to **~1.1e-5** (5 decimal places). The small residual is the fee accrual — `(balance − fee) / totalSupply = 1.133068249`, which lands within **1.3e-6** of the published price. **The documented NAV ÷ supply formula is confirmed.**

### Published yield/rate inputs — YES

https://usyc.docs.hashnote.com/overview/token-price:

```
## Interest Calculation

The USYC Oracle provides principal and interest at each price update to compute the
current APR.

The principal and interest can be used to compute the overnight interest rate. When
forecasting interest, it is important to account for interest that accrues over multiple
days (e.g. a Friday repo generates a total of 3 days of interest).
```

Every price report publishes `principal`, `interest`, `fee`, `balance`, `totalSupply`, **and `nextPrice`** — where `nextPrice` is exactly the forecast used as the post-2pm Effective Subscription Price. Current `nextPrice` = `1.13317668379315`.

The "Friday repo generates 3 days of interest" note is directly observable: round 482 (Monday 2026-08-03) reported `interest` of 876,505.05 on principal 3,007,030,312.05 — **~3× the single-day accrual** of round 483 (291,303.55). Interest accrues on **actual calendar days**, so the numerator is ACT.

### Fees (bears on net yield)

**Source** — https://usyc.docs.hashnote.com/overview/fees · last modified 2025-12-18 · fetched 2026-08-05

| Performance Fee | Subscription Fee | Redemption Fee |
|---|---|---|
| 10% of yield | 4 bps | 3 bps |

Note "10% of yield" is a **performance fee**; there is **no management fee** on AUM (independently corroborated by rwa.xyz: *"10% performance fee and zero management fees"*). Observed live, `fee / interest` = **8.83%** in both of the last two rounds, not exactly 10% — see UNVERIFIED §C.

### Day-count convention — **NOT DOCUMENTED. See UNVERIFIED §B.**

No Circle, Hashnote, or Circle-developer page states ACT/360 vs ACT/365. My arithmetic strongly implies **ACT/360**, but this is **inference, not a citation.**

### It is a Circle/Hashnote-operated oracle using the Chainlink *interface* — not a Chainlink network

**Source** — https://usyc.docs.hashnote.com/overview/token-price · 2026-08-05

```
USYC price is reported to the USYC Oracle, a Chainlink-style smart contract, once per
business day...

### USYC Oracle

The USYC Oracle adheres to the Chainlink aggregator interface.
```

Note the precise wording: **"Chainlink-style"** and **"adheres to the Chainlink aggregator interface."** That is an ABI-compatibility statement, not a Chainlink DON. **I found no evidence of a Chainlink NAVLink feed, no RedStone feed, and no Pyth feed for USYC.** Chainlink does operate NAVLink feeds for other tokenized funds (e.g. `M NAV` at data.chain.link), but I found no USYC one.

# [UNVERIFIED]

Everything below could **not** be confirmed from a primary source. **Do not state any of it as fact on stage.**

### §A — DST behaviour of the 2:00pm cutoff — UNVERIFIED
The two official pages are **internally inconsistent**: Token Price says **"2:00 pm ET"**, Product Structuring says **"2 PM EST"**. Neither addresses daylight saving time. I could not find any Circle/Hashnote statement on whether the cutoff shifts in UTC terms between EST and EDT. My *assumption* (unverified) is that "ET" is intended and the cutoff floats with US Eastern local time — but the docs literally say "EST" in one place. **If asked, say the docs say 2pm Eastern and are not explicit about DST.**

### §B — Day-count convention (ACT/360 vs ACT/365) — UNVERIFIED
**No primary source states it.** Not in the Token Price page, Fees page, Product Structuring page, Circle developer docs, or circle.com/usyc. I could not obtain the SDYF offering memorandum (it is not public; the fund is a Reg-S Cayman mutual fund, so there is no SEC filing to read — see §F).

My inference, from live data, is **ACT/360**:
- The numerator is definitely **ACT** (actual calendar days) — confirmed by the documented "a Friday repo generates a total of 3 days of interest" and observed directly (Monday round 482 accrued ~3× the Tuesday round 483).
- Round 483 gross: interest 291,303.55 / principal 3,007,906,817.13 = 9.68459e-5/day → **ACT/360 = 3.4865%**, ACT/365 = 3.5349%.
- Net of fee, price-implied: **ACT/360 ≈ 3.14%**, ACT/365 ≈ 3.18%.
- rwa.xyz (third-party) reports 7D APY **3.19%** and 30D APY **3.48%**.
- ACT/360 is the standard convention for the fed funds / repo market the fund actually operates in, and Circle's docs say assets "earn the overnight federal funds rate through reverse repo agreements."

This is **strongly suggestive but is arithmetic triangulation, not a citation.** If asked on stage: *"The docs don't publish the day-count; the economics are consistent with ACT/360, which is the repo-market convention — I'd want to confirm against the offering memorandum."*

### §C — The 10% performance fee doesn't reconcile exactly — UNVERIFIED

I could not determine why the realized ratio is 8.83% rather than 10% — possible explanations (all unverified) include a different fee base, a hurdle, or an accrual-timing offset. **Don't quote a realized fee percentage.** Quote the documented headline: 10% performance fee, 4bps subscription, 3bps redemption, no management fee.

The offering memorandum is not public.
