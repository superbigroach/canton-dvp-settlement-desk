What this site is running on. Since 24 September 2026 the values and the public API come from ETP Foundry's own Canton DevNet validator, running the published Daml packages — not from a demonstration sandbox. The participant was approved by the Canton Foundation on 23 September 2026 and holds real registry assets. It is DevNet: it is not TestNet or MainNet, and nothing here settles a real obligation.

What has not happened yet. No committee of third parties has been seated, and no fixing has been attested by one. Values shown as "seed" are the instrument's own reference mark and are not attestations. Where a value is attested by seats ETP Foundry itself operates, it is labelled as a pilot on the value. No fixing has been published for commercial use.

ETP Foundry is not an authorised or registered benchmark administrator in any jurisdiction. Nothing on this site is a price, a valuation, a benchmark for regulatory purposes, investment advice, or an offer of any financial product or service. See Regulatory.
