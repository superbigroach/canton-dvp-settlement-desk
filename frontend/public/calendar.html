Publication calendar

When each number is struck, and when it is not.

A tokenised asset trades every day, so its official close is struck every day — weekends and holidays included. What changes on a holiday is not whether we publish, but which tier can be reached, and the calendar says so in advance.

Official closes are struck at 16:00 Europe/London every calendar day. London observes British Summer Time: the strike stays at 16:00 local — 15:00 UTC until 25 Oct 2026, 16:00 UTC from 26 Oct 2026 to 27 Mar 2027, then 15:00 UTC again. Indicative values are published at 00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC every day (rulebook §5.3, 24/7 methodology).

Notice periods you can plan around

  • Methodology change: 5 business days' notice; 30 calendar days and written notice to every licensee for a material change. Never retroactive.
  • Restatement: only if the error is found the same day and exceeds 0.10 %; otherwise the value stands and the error is listed in the quarterly error table.
  • Cessation: 60 calendar days' notice, enforced on-ledger (CessationNotice).
  • Oversight committee: quarterly; summary minutes published within 10 business days.
  • Quarterly error table: published within 15 business days of quarter end, from the first quarter with a published fixing.

Times are shown in Europe/London unless stated. The authoritative schedule is the one served by /api/benchmarks (publishTime, timezone); this page reads it on load.

What this site is running on. Since 24 September 2026 the values and the public API come from ETP Foundry's own Canton DevNet validator, running the published Daml packages — not from a demonstration sandbox. The participant was approved by the Canton Foundation on 23 September 2026 and holds real registry assets. It is DevNet: it is not TestNet or MainNet, and nothing here settles a real obligation.

What has not happened yet. No committee of third parties has been seated, and no fixing has been attested by one. Values shown as "seed" are the instrument's own reference mark and are not attestations. Where a value is attested by seats ETP Foundry itself operates, it is labelled as a pilot on the value. No fixing has been published for commercial use.
