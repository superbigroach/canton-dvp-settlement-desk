Benchmarks

The CrossDesk fixings.

Every identifier has the form CDX-<INSTRUMENT>-<FREQ>. One fixing per identifier per strike; a second strike for the same date is a restatement, never a second fixing. Each published value carries its tier, its signers, and — when carried forward — the age of the underlying strike.

Reading a value

What sits beside every number.

As of

The strike instant — the moment the committee's quorum was reached, not the moment the transaction committed. Shown in the benchmark's declared time zone.

Attested K of N

Written only when K real signatures exist on the ledger. Below K there is no fixing, and the tile says so.

Tier

Which step of the fallback waterfall produced the value. Tier 1 is a committee-attested strike; higher tiers are flagged fallbacks. See Governance.

Age

How long ago the value was struck. A value older than two days is painted as stale.

Two numbers are published side by side and must never be confused: the official fixing, which creation and redemption settle at, and the indicative value, derived continuously from live market data and binding on nobody. This site shows the official fixing. Machine access: GET /api/benchmarks, GET /api/series/{id} and /api/series/{id}.csv.

Sandbox disclosure. The values on this site come from a hosted sandbox: an in-process Canton ledger running the CrossDesk Daml packages, seeded with a demonstration book. It is not a Canton Network participant and it is not connected to DevNet, TestNet or MainNet. No fixing has been published for commercial use, and no committee has been convened. Instruments in the demonstration basket are issued for demonstration.

CrossDesk is not an authorised or registered benchmark administrator in any jurisdiction. Nothing on this site is a price, a valuation, a benchmark for regulatory purposes, investment advice, or an offer of any financial product or service. See Regulatory.
