About

What this fixing measures.

The price at which the instrument is deemed to transact for settlement purposes at the strike time. It is not an estimate of fair value, a prediction, or an indication of where the instrument may trade next. It is the number contracts settle against.

Attested by

Seats, and what each one asserts.

A fixing needs K of N. No seat is asked for an opinion about the price; each asserts a fact only it can see, by naming the conditions it verified. The composition is opposed by design — the issuer favours par, the lender favours prudence, the venue favours the observed print — and no single interest may hold K. Conditions are served by /api/signer-protocol (SIGNER_PROTOCOL v2), the same list the ledger-facing API accepts.

Recommended N = 5, K = 3; pilot minimum N = 3, K = 2. No committee has yet been convened for this identifier; the seats above describe the protocol, not occupants. See Governance.

    Usage & licensing

    Reading is free. Settling against it is licensed.

    Referencing an ETP Foundry fixing in a contract requires a licence from the administrator; reading a published fixing does not. Three licence types — use, redistribution, display — cover the different rights, and only the first is meaningful money.

    What this site is running on. Since 24 September 2026 the values and the public API come from ETP Foundry's own Canton DevNet validator, running the published Daml packages — not from a demonstration sandbox. The participant was approved by the Canton Foundation on 23 September 2026 and holds real registry assets. It is DevNet: it is not TestNet or MainNet, and nothing here settles a real obligation.

    What has not happened yet. No committee of third parties has been seated, and no fixing has been attested by one. Values shown as "seed" are the instrument's own reference mark and are not attestations. Where a value is attested by seats ETP Foundry itself operates, it is labelled as a pilot on the value. No fixing has been published for commercial use.

    ETP Foundry is not an authorised or registered benchmark administrator in any jurisdiction. Nothing on this site is a price, a valuation, a benchmark for regulatory purposes, investment advice, or an offer of any financial product or service. See Regulatory.
