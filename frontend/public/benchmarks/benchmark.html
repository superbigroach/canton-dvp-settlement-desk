About

What this fixing measures.

The price at which the instrument is deemed to transact for settlement purposes at the strike time. It is not an estimate of fair value, a prediction, or an indication of where the instrument may trade next. It is the number contracts settle against.

Attested by

Seats, and what each one asserts.

A fixing needs K of N. No seat is asked for an opinion about the price; each asserts a fact only it can see, by naming the conditions it verified. The composition is opposed by design — the issuer favours par, the lender favours prudence, the venue favours the observed print — and no single interest may hold K. Conditions are served by /api/signer-protocol (SIGNER_PROTOCOL v1), the same list the ledger-facing API accepts.

Recommended N = 5, K = 3; pilot minimum N = 3, K = 2. No committee has yet been convened for this identifier; the seats above describe the protocol, not occupants. See Governance.

    Usage & licensing

    Reading is free. Settling against it is licensed.

    Referencing a CrossDesk fixing in a contract requires a licence from the administrator; reading a published fixing does not. Three licence types — use, redistribution, display — cover the different rights, and only the first is meaningful money.
