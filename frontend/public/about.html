About

Two roles. Neither is an exchange.

ETP Foundry is the price layer for tokenised funds on the Canton Network: it administers the fixings those funds settle against, and it is the transfer agent through which their shares are created and redeemed at the attested number.

What ETP Foundry is

An administrator, and a transfer agent.

Benchmark administrator

For tokenised assets on Canton that no administrator covers — wrapped assets, baskets, funds whose underlying market is closed. ETP Foundry proposes the fixing at a declared time, has a K-of-N committee of parties with money on the mark attest it, and publishes it with its tier, its signers and the conditions each signer verified. Products: CBTC Close, cETH Close, and fund NAVs such as LX1.

Transfer agent

For funds that reference those fixings: creation and redemption in kind, at the attested NAV, atomically, for authorised participants. Deliver the basket, receive shares — or the reverse — all-or-nothing, with the fee charged in the same transaction. The mechanism that keeps a fund glued to its NAV.

The whole system is a set of Daml packages on Canton, a Spring backend that operates them, and the desk. On Canton a contract is visible only to its signatories, so a sealed order is observed by nobody and a fixing's signatory set is its attestor set — properties the design relies on rather than adds. A sealed closing auction exists as a module for assets with a real market on Canton; it is not on the pilot surface.

Where we sit

Four firms do this today. On a ledger they collapse into one.

The question we are asked most is whether we are a benchmark administrator like CF Benchmarks or ICE. We are that, and three other things, and the clearest way to show it is the service-provider list of the largest bitcoin ETF — where the index provider and the administrator are two different companies doing two different jobs.

What has to happen, every dayWho does it in a traditional fundHere
Publish a defensible price for the asset a benchmark administrator — CF Benchmarks for BlackRock's IBIT, CoinDesk Indices for Grayscale's GBTC, ICE Benchmark Administration for the LBMA Gold Price ETP Foundry
Strike the NAV, keep the books, accrue the fees a fund administrator — BNY Mellon for IBIT and GBTC, Fidelity Service Company for FBTC ETP Foundry
Keep the register and process creations and redemptions a transfer agent — State Street for FBTC, BNY Mellon and Continental Stock Transfer for GBTC ETP Foundry
Hold the assets and settle the swap a custodian — Coinbase Custody and Anchorage for IBIT, Fidelity Digital Assets for FBTC unchanged: the issuer keeps its own custodian. We never hold anyone's assets

Every name and role above is taken from those funds' own prospectuses and annual reports. Bundling is already normal in traditional finance — BNY Mellon is both administrator and transfer agent for GBTC, and Fidelity's index provider, custodian and administrator are all affiliates, disclosed as a conflict. The cure there is disclosure, not separation.

So what is actually different here

Their committee governs. Ours signs.

ICE and CF both have oversight committees. They meet quarterly, review the methodology and read the error log. No committee in traditional finance signs each daily value. The administrator computes it and publishes it. Ours signs every fixing, and the signers are parties who lose money if the number is wrong.

Their method needs a market. Ours does not.

ICE runs a real auction; CF averages trades across named exchanges. Both are better than any committee when the market is open — which for a tokenised equity is about 32 of the 168 hours in a week. When nothing trades, an auction has no bidders and an average has nothing to average. A committee can attest the absence and publish a number with an honest band.

Their record is a document. Ours is a contract.

A traditional administrator's assurance is its name and its minutes. Here the rules are enforced by the ledger: the administrator signs every proposal and can never be a member, at least two members must agree, and a venue must supply a range containing the price or formally attest that nothing traded. Nobody — including us — can manufacture a committee fixing.

One line, then: we are the benchmark administrator, the fund administrator and the transfer agent, in one piece of software, with the number attested by the parties who carry the risk. We are not the issuer, so we never need their licence. We are not the venue, so we never price what we trade. We are not the custodian, so we never hold anyone's assets. That is what makes us safe for all three to use at once.

What ETP Foundry is not

Stated plainly, because the distinctions matter.

Not an exchange

ETP Foundry does not operate a continuous market and does not earn trading fees. A continuous book and a perpetuals layer exist in the code, tested, and neither is sold: operating a market is a licensed activity, and staying out of it is deliberate.

Not a custodian

ETP Foundry holds nobody's assets. Creation and redemption move holdings between the authorised participant and the fund on the ledger; nothing passes through the administrator.

Not a regulated administrator

Benchmark administration is a licensed activity in the UK and EU. ETP Foundry is not authorised, registered or recognised anywhere, and says so on its Regulatory page.

Not a price feed

A feed relays a number that exists. ETP Foundry attests a value where none exists to relay — a wrapper's discount to par, a basket, a fund at 03:00 on a Sunday — under a published method, with every signature attributable.

Not a trader in what it prices

The administrator does not take positions in the instruments it fixes. That is a hard constraint on the business, not a preference.

Live on our own participant, not yet on MainNet

Since 24 September 2026 this site runs on ETP Foundry's own Canton DevNet validator, approved by the Canton Foundation, holding real registry assets. What is still missing is not software: it is a committee of third parties. Until one signs, the published values say so.

What this site is running on. Since 24 September 2026 the values and the public API come from ETP Foundry's own Canton DevNet validator, running the published Daml packages — not from a demonstration sandbox. The participant was approved by the Canton Foundation on 23 September 2026 and holds real registry assets. It is DevNet: it is not TestNet or MainNet, and nothing here settles a real obligation.

What has not happened yet. No committee of third parties has been seated, and no fixing has been attested by one. Values shown as "seed" are the instrument's own reference mark and are not attestations. Where a value is attested by seats ETP Foundry itself operates, it is labelled as a pilot on the value. No fixing has been published for commercial use.
